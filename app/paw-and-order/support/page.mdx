# Support

## Playing with your cat

- Put the iPad flat on the floor, screen up, with the brightness up.
- Pick a level on the start screen: tap a card, check the vermin and their points, then tap **Enter**. Or tap the seal to jump straight onto the Herald Square subway platform.
- Let your cat watch first. Most cats start pawing within a minute.
- Turn on **Guided Access** (Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access) so a paw can't leave the app.

## Controls for humans

In a level, there's one control: the handle at the bottom middle. Pull it up to the top of its track to pause and open the menu. A paw resting on it won't trigger it, and a quick tap shows a hint.

The menu has:

- **Sound**: turns all sound on or off.
- **Sound effects** and **Background ambience**: a switch and a volume for each.
- **Resume**: back to play.
- **Leave level**: back to the start screen.

## Common questions

**My cat isn't interested.** Try a darker room, a brighter screen, and the Midtown or Herald Square level. Cats react most to fast, stop-and-go movement. Short sessions work better than long ones.

**Is it safe for the screen?** Keep your cat's claws trimmed, or use a screen protector.

**Where's my cat's play history?** It stays on your iPad and is never uploaded.

**How do I restore a purchase?** If the app has a purchase, open the unlock screen and tap **Restore purchase**.
