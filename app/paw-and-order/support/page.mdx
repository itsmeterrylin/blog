## Playing with your cat

- Put the iPad flat on the floor, screen up, with the brightness up.
- Pick a level on the start screen: tap a card, check the vermin and their points, then tap **Enter**. Or tap the seal to jump straight into the Subway.
- Let your cat watch first. Most cats start pawing within a minute.
- Turn on **Guided Access** (Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access) so a paw can't leave the app.

## Controls for humans

In a level, the three corner controls are slides: drag the knob along its track to the end.

- **Back** (top left) returns to the start screen.
- **Sound** (top right) turns all sound on or off.
- **Settings** (top right) opens volume controls for effects and background ambience.

A quick tap shows a hint. A paw resting on a control won't trigger it.

## Common questions

**My cat isn't interested.** Try a darker room, a brighter screen, and the Times Square or Subway level. Cats react most to fast, stop-and-go movement. Short sessions work better than long ones.

**Is it safe for the screen?** Keep your cat's claws trimmed, or use a screen protector.

**Where's my cat's play history?** It stays on your iPad and is never uploaded.

**How do I restore a purchase?** If the app has a purchase, open the unlock screen and tap **Restore purchase**.
