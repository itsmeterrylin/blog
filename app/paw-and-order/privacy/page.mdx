# Privacy Policy

*Paw & Order: Special Vermin Unit · Last updated October 10, 2026*

Paw & Order does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## What the app keeps

The app saves two things on your iPad, and nothing leaves it:

- **Play history.** Each session's level, length, score, and catches, so the game can show progress. It's stored in the app's own folder.
- **Settings.** Sound on or off, and the effects and ambience volumes.

You can delete both at any time by deleting the app.

## What the app doesn't do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- No network requests during play.
- No camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos access.

## Purchases

If the app offers an in-app purchase, Apple processes it. The app never sees your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Links

The start screen has a link to the developer's YouTube channel, behind a slide so a cat can't open it. YouTube's own privacy policy applies once you leave the app.

## Children

The app is made for cats and the adults who set it up. It isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
