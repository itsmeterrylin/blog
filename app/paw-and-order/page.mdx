# Paw & Order: Special Vermin Unit

An iPad game for cats. Rats, cockroaches, pizza rats, moths, and koi scurry, hop, and swim across nine New York places, from Midtown to a Halloween stoop. Your cat paws them, and they flatten into cartoon splats.

- **Made for paws.** Big targets, several paws at once, and no menus during play. The few controls for humans are slides a paw can't trigger.
- **Nine places.** Midtown, the NQRW platform, the Great Lawn, Hester St., a rooftop, Coney Island, a grocery store, Bethesda Fountain, and Halloween.
- **No data collected.** No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Made in NYC at [Terry's Apartment](https://www.youtube.com/@terrysapartment).
